13-L-054 y=659.1-669.1 a longer duration threshold—for example, 200 msec—

14-L-001 y=76.1-86.1 9. Summary
14-L-002 y=87.1-97.1 This study was undertaken to determine the criticality
14-L-003 y=98.1-108.1 of the setting of the dispersion threshold in a dispersion-
14-L-004 y=109.1-119.1 based algorithm for fixation detection. The study was also
14-L-005 y=120.1-130.1 an attempt to determine the optimum dispersion threshold
14-L-006 y=131.1-141.1 for each of five different measures of dispersion.
14-L-007 y=142.1-152.1 The radius of fixations was used as a common dis-
14-L-008 y=153.1-163.1 persion measure across the various metrics. A 1º radius
14-L-009 y=164.1-174.1 threshold would, for example, be equivalent to a disper-
14-L-010 y=175.1-185.1 sion threshold of 0.24º for the SD metric.
14-L-011 y=186.1-196.1 The hypothesis that the number, position, size, and du-
14-L-012 y=197.1-207.1 ration of fixations that are identified are functions of the
14-L-013 y=208.1-218.1 metric used for dispersion proved to hold in most respects.
14-L-014 y=219.1-229.1 The number of fixations identified by the DT metric was
14-L-015 y=230.1-240.1 significantly lower than that identified by the other met-
14-L-016 y=241.1-251.1 rics, whereas the duration of fixations identified by the
14-L-017 y=252.1-262.1 DD and DT metrics were generally longer than those iden-
14-L-018 y=263.1-273.1 tified by the Rd, Slv, and SD metrics. The DT and Rd met-
14-L-019 y=274.1-284.1 rics led to fixations that were smaller than those identified
14-L-020 y=285.1-295.1 by the DD, Slv, and SD metrics. The DT and SD metrics
14-L-021 y=296.1-306.1 returned scan paths that differed somewhat from those re-
14-L-022 y=307.1-317.1 turned by the DD, Slv, and Rd metrics, indicating that the
14-L-023 y=318.1-328.1 position of fixations is also a function of the metric used.
14-L-024 y=329.1-339.1 The fact that the number, position, size, and duration of
14-L-025 y=340.1-350.1 fixations that are identified are functions of the threshold
14-L-026 y=351.1-361.1 value could always have been accepted as obvious, but
14-L-027 y=362.1-372.1 definite trends were identified in each case. The size and
14-L-028 y=373.1-383.1 duration of fixations are linear functions of the threshold
14-L-029 y=384.1-394.1 value, and the number of fixations reaches a maximum at
14-L-030 y=395.1-405.1 some threshold, whereafter it decreases linearly with the
14-L-031 y=406.1-416.1 threshold. The position of fixations also depends on the
14-L-032 y=417.1-427.1 threshold, as indicated by the LA that is also maximized at
14-L-033 y=428.1-438.1 some threshold value.
14-L-034 y=439.1-449.1 It was found that there is no definite optimum disper-
14-L-035 y=450.1-460.1 sion threshold to be used in a dispersion-based fixation
14-L-036 y=461.1-471.1 detection algorithm but that the various indicators point
14-L-037 y=472.1-482.1 to an acceptable range for the radius threshold between
14-L-038 y=483.1-493.1 0.7º and 1.3º. Within this range, the LA between metrics
14-L-039 y=494.1-504.1 is reasonably constant at about 90%. A lower threshold
14-L-040 y=505.1-515.1 within this range will ensure more fixations with PORs
14-L-041 y=516.1-526.1 that are less clustered within fixations, whereas a larger
14-L-042 y=527.1-537.1 threshold will utilize more PORs. Using threshold val-
14-L-043 y=538.1-548.1 ues less than 0.7º will result in fixation sequences that
14-L-044 y=549.1-559.1 differ considerably from one another. Threshold values
14-L-045 y=560.1-570.1 above 1.3º are less of a danger, but since longer and larger
14-L-046 y=571.1-581.1 fixations will be identified with PORs that can be more
14-L-047 y=582.1-592.1 clustered within fixations, it is possible that fixations will
14-L-048 y=593.1-603.1 be reported at positions that differ from a viewer’s actual
14-L-049 y=604.1-614.1 point of attention.
14-L-050 y=615.1-625.1 The 0.7º–1.3º range for the radius threshold is equiva-
14-L-051 y=626.1-636.1 lent to a very small range of 0.17º–0.30º for the SD metric.
14-L-052 y=637.1-647.1 The margin of error for the DD metric is somewhat bigger,
14-L-053 y=648.1-658.1 with an acceptable range of 1.1º–2.0º.
14-L-054 y=659.1-669.1 The fact that the correct setting of the dispersion thresh-
14-L-055 y=670.1-680.1 old is quite critical and that the number and position of
14-L-056 y=681.1-691.1 fixations that are identified are dependent on the set-
14-L-057 y=692.1-702.1 ting has important implications for eyetracking research.
14-L-058 y=703.1-713.1 Researchers should be aware of the exact metric used in
14-L-059 y=714.1-724.1 the tools that they are using and should set the threshold
14-L-060 y=725.1-735.1 accordingly.
